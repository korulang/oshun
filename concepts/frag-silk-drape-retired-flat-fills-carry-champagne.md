The pearlescent silk drape (instanced kind 4) was retired from the
material vocabulary — not tuned. Its thin-film hue fringe cycled ~4
palette periods across the normal range, which read as dense moiré
contour banding on the flat panels the surface actually draws. Two
tuning passes confirmed the banding is the material's character, not a
parameter error: kill it, don't adjust it.

The champagne identity does not depend on the drape. Every former silk
call site draws a flat roundrect in the same champagne tones and the
cards still read as ours. "Premium" survives on shadow, hairline
borders, measured type, and motion — the pieces that were doing the
work anyway.

The shader body is preserved in git history (pre-f566041, canvas/index.kz
kind-4 branch + silk_field/silk_pal helpers) should a band-limited
resurrection be wanted — the honest fix would be fwidth-based fade of
the hue fringe, not constant reduction. Glass (kind 5) and frost
(kind 6) are untouched and carry the fancy-material slot.
